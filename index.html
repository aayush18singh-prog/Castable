🤖 AI Casting Insight

Arjun's screen energy reads restrained intensity — high emotional depth with controlled delivery. This positions him strongly for lead and co-lead roles in OTT drama and thriller formats. His natural Hindi diction and urban look type are well-suited for Amazon Prime and SonyLIV original productions. His comedic timing scores lower (58) — not a weakness, but a signal to target dramatic rather than ensemble comedy roles. Recommended first move: Target supporting roles in JioCinema or Prime dramas before pursuing lead cinema.

Vision Analysis Multimodal AI

Face expressiveness, emotion range from self-tape, look-type classification (protagonist / character / supporting), physical archetype tagging. Reads micro-expressions across every frame.

Voice & Speech Analysis Audio ML

Tonality (warm / commanding / soft), language accent detection, dialect purity, speech pace and resonance. Scores for voiceover suitability, regional cinema fit, and ad film narration potential.

Screen Presence Scoring Proprietary

Measures stillness vs energy balance, eye contact, spatial ownership on frame, and command of the lens — the intangible quality casting directors call "it factor," made measurable.

Role ↔ Talent Matching Vector Embeddings

Semantic match between role brief and castability profile. Understands nuance — "subtle intensity for a SonyLIV thriller" vs "raw energy for a JioCinema action series."

Growth Tracking Longitudinal

Tracks actor across re-submissions. "Your comedic timing improved 12% vs last tape. Your dramatic range is now in the top 18% of Hindi OTT actors on the platform."
